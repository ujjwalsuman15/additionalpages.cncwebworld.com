What is Data Science?

Data Science combines programming, statistics, mathematics and domain knowledge to extract useful insights from data and support data-driven decisions.

Our Data Science course is designed to take learners from programming and data handling fundamentals to exploratory data analysis, machine learning and real-world project implementation.

The focus is not only on theory. You will work with datasets, solve practical problems, build models and learn how to present your findings.
