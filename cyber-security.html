What Will You Learn?

Cybersecurity focuses on protecting computers, networks, applications, data and users from security threats. A strong foundation combines networking, operating systems, security principles and practical analysis.

This course begins with networking and Linux fundamentals and progresses through security controls, vulnerability assessment, web security, incident response and digital forensics.

Practical activities are designed for authorized lab environments and emphasize responsible, legal and ethical security testing.
